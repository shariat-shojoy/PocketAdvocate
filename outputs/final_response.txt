The relevant laws in this case are:

- Section 44 of The Penal Code, 1860, which defines "injury" and can be relevant in understanding the scope of the threat.
- Section 190 of The Penal Code, 1860, which discusses the threat of injury to induce a person to refrain from applying for protection to a public servant, may not directly apply but is related to the concept of threats.

These laws may apply because the threat made by the sender falls under the definition of criminal intimidation as per Section 503 of The Penal Code, 1860. The threat is intended to cause alarm and potentially force the recipient to act in a certain way to avoid the threatened injury. Section 44 helps in understanding what constitutes an injury, which is relevant in this context. Although Section 190 is more specific to refraining from applying for protection, it shows the legal system's stance on threats of injury.
